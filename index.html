FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and creating a shorter, condensed version that redirects to the original URL. When a user clicks on the shortened link, they are redirected to the intended destination.

Is it necessary to create an account to use the URL shortening service?

No, you can shorten links right away without an account. However, creating a free account gives you access to link history, custom aliases, QR codes, and performance analytics.

Are the shortened links permanent? Will they expire?

All standard shortened links created on Scissor are permanent and will not expire unless manually archived or deleted from your dashboard.

Are there any limitations on the number of URLs I can shorten?

Our free tier offers generous link generation limits. For high-volume business needs or automated API generation, our Professional and Teams plans provide unlimited link creation.

Can I customize the shortened URLs to reflect my brand or content?

Yes! With Scissor, you can specify custom slugs (e.g. scissor.app/my-campaign) as well as connect your own custom branded domain.

Can I track the performance of my shortened URLs?

Yes. Our analytics dashboard provides deep insight into click counts, geographic location, referrers, and device types in real time.

How secure is the URL shortening service? Are the shortened links protected against spam or malicious activity?

We actively monitor and scan all shortened destinations against global threat databases, phishing registries, and malware lists to protect your audience.

What is a QR code and what can it do?

A QR code is a machine-readable barcode that opens your shortened link instantly with smartphone cameras. Perfect for flyers, business cards, billboards, and presentations.

Is there an API available for integrating the URL shortening service into my own applications or websites?

Yes, Scissor offers a robust REST API with documentation, client SDKs, and webhook notifications for automated enterprise workflows.
